What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our platform. They contain information about your browsing activity and preferences, helping us enhance your experience and understand how you use our site.

Note: You cannot disable essential cookies—the platform won't function without them. You can manage non-essential cookies through your preferences.

Managing Your Cookies

Cookie Banner

When you first visit, a banner appears explaining our cookie use.

  • ✓ Accept All - Allow all cookies
  • ✓ Reject All - Disable non-essential cookies
  • ✓ Customize - Choose which cookies to accept

Browser Controls

Control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
